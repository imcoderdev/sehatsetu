How It Works

📱 Offline-First

ASHA workers can register patients even without internet. Data syncs automatically when connection returns.

🤖 AI Triage

Google Gemini AI analyzes symptoms and assigns urgency — RED, YELLOW, or GREEN priority.

📋 Doctor Queue

Doctors see a live prioritized patient queue with one-tap calling and note-taking.

💬 SMS Intake

Patients without smartphones can text symptoms via SMS and get added to the doctor queue automatically.

💊 Pharmacy Stock

Real-time medicine availability prevents doctors from prescribing out-of-stock medicines.

🔒 Secure

JWT authentication for doctors and pharmacists. Row-level security on all patient data.
